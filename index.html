PhD Research Agenda

Designing the digital future for emerging states

Proposed Research Title

"Designing Secure and Scalable Digital Governance Architectures for Emerging States"

Research Problem

Many emerging states lack integrated, secure, and interoperable digital governance infrastructures. There is often a structural disconnect between ICT system implementation and governance frameworks.

Research Objectives

  • Develop a scalable e-governance architecture model
  • Integrate cybersecurity frameworks into national public systems
  • Design interoperability standards across ministries
  • Propose a policy-technology alignment framework

Methodology

  • Comparative analysis of EU digital governance systems
  • Systems architecture modeling
  • Cybersecurity risk assessment
  • Prototype framework development
  • Case study validation

Expected Contribution

  • A digital governance reference architecture model
  • A cybersecurity-integrated public systems blueprint
  • An adaptable framework for developing and post-conflict states
